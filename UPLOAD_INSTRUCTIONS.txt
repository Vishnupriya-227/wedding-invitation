SARANAMS WEDDING INVITATION — DEPLOYMENT

1. Upload the contents of this folder to the root of your hosting site, keeping `index.html` at that root.
2. Keep `Meghaallo.mp3` beside `index.html` so the background music can load.
3. Configure your hosting provider to publish from the correct branch and folder.
4. After deployment, open the live page in a private/incognito browser and confirm the invitation, event dates, countdown, and map links.

The countdown and background music are included. Photo galleries, guest uploads, and RSVP are not currently included. Photos and design customizations will be added later.
